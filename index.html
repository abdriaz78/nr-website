About Us

Manufacturing Trust, One Bonded Part at a Time

NR Rubber Parts is a Pakistan-based manufacturer of automotive rubber components and rubber-to-metal bonded parts, serving distributors and workshops with a catalog spanning over 1,400 part numbers across 17+ vehicle makes — from Suzuki and Toyota to Honda, Nissan, Mitsubishi, Hyundai, Kia and more.

Every component is engineered to OEM size, fit, form and function, giving mechanics and parts retailers a dependable, locally-manufactured alternative without compromising on performance.
